Status: LOCKED. 14 buckets = candidate 13 with the social bucket split
paid vs organic (JC). SEO (organic) and SEA (paid) were already separate.

| # | Bucket | Membership rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SEO & organische vindbaarheid | Organic findability in search engines or AI answers (seo, linkbuilding, geo, "chatgpt geciteerd"); no paid placement. |
| 2 | SEA, display & overige paid advertising | Paid ads bought on search engines, display/programmatic networks, or paid advertising naming no platform at all; paid ads on a named social platform go to bucket 4. |
| 3 | Social media organisch | Organic social work: content, beheer, community, influencer-op-eigen-kanaal. DEFAULT when the text says only "social media" with no paid marker. |
| 4 | Social media paid | ONLY when the text carries a paid marker: social ads, adverteren op, paid social, meta/tiktok/linkedin ads, campagnebudget. |
| 5 | Website & CMS | Building, redesigning or optimizing a website (not a shop) or the CMS/framework it runs on (wordpress, webflow, umbraco, drupal, laravel). |
| 6 | Webshop & e-commerceplatform | An online store, or the commerce platform it is built or migrated on (shopify, woocommerce, magento, shopware, lightspeed, commercetools). |
| 7 | E-mail, CRM & marketing automation | Direct one-to-one digital messaging (email, whatsapp, sms) or the CRM/automation platform driving it (klaviyo, hubspot, activecampaign, pipedrive). |
| 8 | Content, video & PR | Production of audiovisual/audio/photo/editorial content, livestreams & streaming platforms, or press/earned media, with no distribution platform named. |
| 9 | Offline media & events | Any non-digital channel: tv, radio, print, direct mail (post), out-of-home, beurzen/events. |
| 10 | Apps & custom platforms | A custom-built application, software platform or interactive technology (apps, ar/vr, api-koppelingen, booking engines) beyond a standard website or webshop. |
| 11 | Marktplaatsen & feeds | Selling or advertising on third-party marketplaces (bol, amazon) or managing the product feeds supplying them (channable). |
| 12 | Branding, campagne & activatie | Brand-, creative- or campaign-level work where no delivery medium is named; includes employer branding & recruitment marketing. |
| 13 | Data, tracking & CRO | Measurement, tracking, analytics or conversion-rate optimization work. |
| 14 | Multi-channel & omnichannel | Explicitly generic whole-of-digital phrasing (online marketing, omnichannel) OR an enumeration spanning 3+ buckets with none primary. |
