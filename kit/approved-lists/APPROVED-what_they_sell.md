# APPROVED — what they sell (JC, Aug 7)

Status: LOCKED. 18 buckets = candidate 18 + Outcome-verkoop (JC) - Events & beurzen (JC excluded, Aug 7: event phrasings fall to unknown).
Note kept on purpose: channel-named services (SEO, ads, social) stay here —
this list is the SERVICE delivered; the platform/channel list is WHERE it ran.
One case can be tagged on both.

| # | Bucket | Membership rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Website & webdesign | Deliverable is a company website — design, build, hosting or maintenance; not a shop, not custom software. |
| 2 | Webshop & e-commerce | Deliverable is an online store or e-commerce platform (build, redesign, migration, shop integrations). |
| 3 | Branding & huisstijl | Deliverable is the brand itself: identity, name, logo, huisstijl, brand positioning or brand strategy. |
| 4 | Employer branding & recruitment | The audience of the deliverable is potential employees, whatever the format (video, campaign, site). |
| 5 | SEO & vindbaarheid | Deliverable is organic search visibility; no paid placement. |
| 6 | Online adverteren (paid ads) | Deliverable is buying/managing paid ad placements on any channel (Google, Meta, display, retargeting). |
| 7 | Campagnes & creatieve concepten | Deliverable is a campaign or creative concept aimed at customers/public — the idea plus its rollout, not one ad account. |
| 8 | Social media content & beheer | Deliverable is creating or managing organic social presence; no paid placement. |
| 9 | Content & copywriting | Deliverable is written/editorial content or a content program (words, not moving image). |
| 10 | Video, animatie & fotografie | Deliverable is produced audio-visual material (film, animation, 3D, photo, audio) not aimed at recruiting staff. |
| 11 | Grafisch ontwerp, drukwerk & print | Deliverable is a designed printed/physical item or the graphic design/DTP work for it. |
| 12 | Apps & maatwerk platforms | Deliverable is custom software: app, portal, platform, integration or system beyond a standard site or shop. |
| 13 | UX/UI & interface design | The phrasing names UX, UI, interface or interaction design (or research/prototyping thereof) as the thing sold. |
| 14 | E-mailmarketing, automation & funnels | Deliverable is an email/CRM/automation/funnel system or its ongoing execution. |
| 15 | Online marketing, performance & CRO | Generic ACTIVITY labels (online marketing, performance marketing, CRO); outcome-named phrasings go to Outcome-verkoop. |
| 16 | Strategie & advies | Deliverable is a plan, advice or research rather than an executed asset; brand positioning excluded (that is Branding). |
| 17 | PR & influencer marketing | Deliverable is earned third-party visibility: press, publicity or influencer attention. |
| 18 | Outcome-verkoop (leads, omzet, groei) | The phrase names the RESULT sold, not the activity: leadgeneratie, meer omzet/aanvragen, groeipartner, "wij leveren klanten". (JC, Aug 7 — counts who sells outcomes vs deliverables.) |
