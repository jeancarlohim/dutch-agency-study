## The numbers for the article

- Narrowing → growth: **null in ~30 cuts** (tests, size bands, categories,
  confound grid, message-only, JC's stuck-profile prediction).
- ≥1 case with a tangible result: **44% growing vs 26% without (+19pp,
  p=0.0015)**. Outside performance agencies: +21pp. Small agencies: 24% vs 8%.
- **91% of 31,868 case studies contain no number.** ~1.5% name revenue.
- Kind of proof: money metrics top (54% growing), reach/views bottom (32%) —
  exploratory ladder, label as such.
- Young agencies: 40% growing vs 27% for 15y+.
- The Sprint core segment: **124 agencies** ≤15 people, flat, showing 4+
  clients, zero numbers (median 3 people, 12 clients shown).
- Dapper: B2B-focused wall of 29 published results (July deep-read), 0
  named-client case studies — "not measurable" under narrowness v2, never
  "scattered".
